Privacy What the game knows about you, and where it sends it

The game sends nothing anywhere. No accounts, no analytics, no crash reports, no advertising. Your position, your body’s figures and where your shelter stands never leave the phone — that is not a marketing promise but a consequence of the game having no servers at all.

What is collected

Nothing is sent, so nothing is collected

ARLS-ZA has no user accounts, no server of its own, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no crash reporting. There is nowhere for your data to be sent, and nobody on the other end to receive it.

Everything the game knows — your character’s height, weight, age and sex, your position while you play, the route the simulation derived from it, your shelter, your inventory and your journal — is written to a database file inside the app’s private storage on your own device.

That file is excluded from Android’s automatic cloud backup (allowBackup=false), specifically so that the location of the place you live is not copied off the phone by the operating system.

Location

Why the game reads your position

The game is played by walking. Your position is what moves the character, what burns their calories and water, what decides whether a search can be finished, and what the map draws. Without it there is no game to play.

Position is read while you play and, if you allow it, while the app is in the background — a survival game that stops counting the moment the screen goes dark would tell you that you walked no further than your last glance at the phone. Background access is optional: refusing it leaves the game fully playable, counting your walk when you next open it.

Position is used on the device and stored on the device. It is never transmitted, never shared with third parties and never sold. There is no mechanism in the app that could do any of those things.

Permissions

What each Android permission is for

Fine and coarse location — moving your character and drawing the map.

Background location — continuing to count your walk with the screen off. Optional, and the game works without it.

Foreground service, with location — the notification Android requires while the game counts a walk in the background. It is what stops the system killing the count mid-walk.

Notifications — telling you that something in the game changed while you were away.

Network

The one thing that leaves the phone

The game downloads offline map packs so that it can draw your city with no connection at all. That download is an ordinary file request to the address listed in the app’s map catalogue and, like any download, it tells the host serving the file that some device asked for it, including its IP address.

Nothing about your character, your position or your play is attached to that request. There is no identifier, no cookie and no second request. Once a pack is downloaded the game runs with no network at all.

Your data

Deleting it

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored, because everything it stored lives inside the app’s own private storage. There is no copy anywhere else to request, export or erase, and no account to close.

Under the GDPR you have rights of access, rectification and erasure over personal data held about you. Here there is no personal data held by anybody but you, on your own device, and you can delete it at any moment.

Age

Who the game is for

ARLS-ZA is not directed at children. It asks a player to walk around a real city, at times including after dark, and its safety rules assume an adult judgement about whether a place and an hour are safe.

Changes

If this policy changes

A change here follows a change in what the app does, never the other way round. The date at the top of this page is when the current version took effect; earlier versions are in the site’s git history.
